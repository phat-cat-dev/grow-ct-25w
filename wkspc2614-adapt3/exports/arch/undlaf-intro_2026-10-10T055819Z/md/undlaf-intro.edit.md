<p>Three things shape how you get on at university: how you learn, how you are assessed, and the feedback you get along the way. Most students meet these one at a time and treat them as separate hurdles. This course takes a different view. It shows how learning, assessment and feedback work together as one cycle, and why seeing that connection is what lets you study with purpose rather than just keep up.</p><p>Over five short lessons you will look at what learning at university actually asks of you, and at how university spells out what you are meant to learn. You will then look at what assessment is really for, at how to make feedback worth having, and finally at how the three connect into a single cycle you can run deliberately. None of it is abstract: it all applies to the work you are already doing, this term, in whatever you are studying. By the end you will have a clearer picture of how your own studies work, and a few practical habits for making each piece of work feed the next.</p>
