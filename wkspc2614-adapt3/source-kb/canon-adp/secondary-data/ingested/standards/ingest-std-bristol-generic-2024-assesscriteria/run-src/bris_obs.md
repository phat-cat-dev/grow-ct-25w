
#### AC7.3 Our reading
<!-- label: editorial; status: drafted; basis: primary (from AC5 to AC7) -->
- **Deficit words stop at the pass line.** "No", "not", "without", gaps and inaccuracies fill the two fail bands; from 40-49 up the only negatives are "some weaknesses" and "minor lapses", and from 50-59 none at all. Bristol describes passing work by what it does, failing work by what it lacks.
- **One degree ladder, read from counts.** very limited (0-19), little and limited (20-39), some and sufficient (40-49), sound, clear, good, consistent (50-69), very good, thorough, effective, excellent (70-79), exceptional, outstanding, highly (80-100). "Some" is the pass word: 31 of its 49 uses are at 40-49.
- **A second ladder for independence.** Even with guidance (fail), with external guidance (40-59, level 4), with limited or minimum guidance, with a degree of autonomy (70-79), then autonomously, with a high degree of autonomy, initiative and setting own goals (80-100). It runs inside each level and climbs again across levels: level 4's pass needs outside guidance, level 6's minimal guidance.
- **Levels move the object, bands move the qualifier.** The same band asks for more as the level rises because the object changes: directed reading (level 4), reading, research and primary sources (levels 5 and 6), current research and advanced scholarship (level 7); introductory knowledge, then well-established principles, then the forefront, then a systematic, specialised field. Within a level the object stays and the qualifier climbs.
- **The grids reuse the FHEQ's own words.** 63 cells share a run of seven or more words with an FHEQ statement. Level 4's argument criterion carries 4.10's "lines of argument ... in accordance with basic theories and concepts"; level 5 carries 4.12's established principles and their development, and applying principles outside their first context; level 7 carries 4.17's forefront and current research. At level 6, the use-of-evidence and argument criteria are Annex D's classification descriptions band for band: 40-49 matches the third, 50-59 the 2.2, 60-69 the 2.1, 80-100 the first. The marking scale is married to the UK classes by wording, not only by marks.
- **Professional and life skills barely change by level.** Teamwork, insight, communication and self-management repeat almost word for word from level 4 to level 7; only decision-making (levels 6 and 7) is new. These criteria grade by band, not by level.
- **Two bands share a text.** Communication's 60-69 and 70-79 cells are identical at every level; a marker cannot tell them apart from the descriptor.
- **Level 7 is a different scale.** Its pass mark is 50, its two lowest bands merge (0-39), and its 40-49 band is a fail; its criteria regroup (argument and explanation merge; specialist skills and research merge).
- **Verbs alone do not carry the level.** "Demonstrate", "use" and "evaluate" appear across all bands; Bloom and SOLO placements spread over several categories (AC6), as the INGEST documents warn. The level is carried by object and scope words, the band by degree and independence words.
