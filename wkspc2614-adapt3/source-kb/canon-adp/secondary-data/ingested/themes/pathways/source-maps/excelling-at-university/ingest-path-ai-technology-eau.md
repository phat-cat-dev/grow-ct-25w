# How *Excelling at University* plugs into AI and Technology

The model authors this pathway from sector and institutional sources: the guide has no chapter on it. This map records where the guide touches AI, so a course can reuse those passages as examples, not as teaching. Pages are the guide's; descriptions are ing's paraphrase.

**Chapters:** none.

## 1. Where the guide touches AI

| Pages | What is there |
|---|---|
| p. 6 | Referencing matters more now that many students are considering how to use generative AI |
| pp. 145-148 | A worked essay example on the impact of generative AI on learning in higher education (introduction and body), used to teach argument and introductions |
| p. 184 | The academic integrity chapter's opening: generative AI makes understanding integrity vital |

## 2. By capability

| Capability | Pages | What the guide teaches | Worked examples and tasks | Notes and gaps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ai-technology/01` (developed) Exercise AI literacy through informed, justified decisions about when, and when not, to use AI. | - | nothing | the AI essay example argues a position on AI in learning (pp. 145-148) | no teaching |
| `ai-technology/03` (developed) Apply a critical-use workflow treating AI as a critical friend, not a replacement for independent thought. | - | nothing | the essay example mentions comparing an AI search engine with a discovery service (p. 148) | no teaching |
| `ai-technology/05` (developed) Weigh the costs of AI/technology use, including environmental and professional impact, against its benefits. | - | nothing | - | no teaching |
| `ai-technology/02` (extended) Avoid GenAI misconduct risks and never cite GenAI outputs as a source. | p. 184 | integrity in the age of generative AI, in the referencing chapter's opening only | - | the referencing pathway's material; no AI-specific rules |
| `ai-technology/06` (extended) Distinguish permitted technological support from an unfair academic advantage. | - | nothing | - | no teaching |
| `ai-technology/04` (unplaced) Progress through tiers of digital/AI capability while treating ethics as cross-cutting. | - | not applicable | - | a design note, not a skill (core Part E item 3) |

## 3. What the guide does not cover

Everything in the pathway's tiers: how generative AI works, its limits, the golden rule, the critical-use workflow, disclosure and the misconduct risks. The guide's essay example is a resource for Academic Writing and, as an argued position, a discussion starter here.
