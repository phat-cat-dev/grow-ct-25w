## 3. What the guide does not cover

The model's Intermediate tier (setbacks, perfectionism, seeking support early) has no section of its own: the guide touches disappointment in Tom and Saeideh (pp. 17-18), support in Saeideh asking for help (p. 18) and psychological capital (p. 249), and perfectionism only in writing (Chapter 9). The move from school or college has one paragraph (p. 44). Independence, self-direction and lifelong learning at Advanced appear in passing (pp. 44, 246). Tinto's and Bridges's accounts of transition and persistence are not in the guide.
