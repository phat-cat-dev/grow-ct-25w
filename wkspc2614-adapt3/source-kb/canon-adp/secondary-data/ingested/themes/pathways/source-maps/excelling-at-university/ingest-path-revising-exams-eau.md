## 3. What the guide does not cover

No capability covers building the revision strategy at Intermediate, though the guide teaches it fully (pp. 235-237; core Part E item 3). The guide cites no research for its techniques; retrieval practice and spacing, the best-evidenced techniques, are present only as self-testing and short sessions. The claim about upward eye movements (p. 240) is unsourced and not drawn on.
