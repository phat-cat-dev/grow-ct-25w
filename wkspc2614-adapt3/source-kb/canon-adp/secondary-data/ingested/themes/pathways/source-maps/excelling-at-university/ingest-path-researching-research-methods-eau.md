# How *Excelling at University* plugs into Researching and Research Methods

The core is generic and cites the guide only as the source of the model's claims; this map says where the guide teaches each part of the pathway (N-0012). Pages and section names are the guide's; descriptions are ing's paraphrase, never extracts of more than 25 words. Capability ids are the model's, or ing's temporary ones (`/cN`). Where the guide has no content for a capability, the row says so.

**Chapters:** Chapter 9's genres (research proposal pp. 150-157, literature review pp. 157-160, reports pp. 160-161); research and the handling of data in Chapter 10 (pp. 187-188); the dissertation in Chapter 5 (p. 66).
