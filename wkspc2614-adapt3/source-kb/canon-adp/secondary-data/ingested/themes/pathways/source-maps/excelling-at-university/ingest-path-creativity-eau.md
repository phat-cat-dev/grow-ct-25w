# How *Excelling at University* plugs into Creativity

The model authors this pathway from an institutional guide and sector sources: the guide has no chapter on it. This map records where the guide touches creativity, so a course can reuse those passages as examples, not as teaching. Pages are the guide's; descriptions are ing's paraphrase.

**Chapters:** none.

## 1. Where the guide touches creativity

| Pages | What is there |
|---|---|
| p. 14 | Creativity, as thinking innovatively to solve problems, among today's graduate attributes |
| p. 65 | Tutors design innovative assessment beyond traditional genres |
| pp. 116-120 | John's research on creativity and imagination in primary music education: the worked example for searching and for analysis and evaluation (Higgins, Trivedi) |

## 2. By capability

| Capability | Pages | What the guide teaches | Worked examples and tasks | Notes and gaps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `creativity/01` (introduced) Recognise creativity as a developable, cross-disciplinary academic skill related to critical thinking. | p. 14 | creativity as a graduate attribute | - | named, not taught |
| `creativity/03` (introduced) Overcome creative barriers such as fear of failure and perfectionism. | - | nothing | - | no teaching |
| `creativity/02` (developed) Apply divergent and convergent thinking at the right stage of a task. | - | nothing | - | no teaching |
| `creativity/04` (developed) Use structured creative problem-solving methods. | - | nothing | - | no teaching; mind mapping is taught as a revision technique (p. 239) |
| `creativity/05` (developed) Exercise cognitive flexibility and learning agility when a strategy is not working. | - | nothing | - | amending a strategy that is not working is taught for self-regulated learning (pp. 56-57) |
| `creativity/07` (developed) Draw on established creativity theory to justify a creative approach. | pp. 118-120 | nothing on creativity theory; the research example's discourse on creativity and imagination | John's example (pp. 116-120) | the example is about searching and critique |
| `creativity/06` (extended) Apply creativity and enterprise thinking to professional and entrepreneurial contexts. | p. 14 | creativity among employers' valued attributes | - | no teaching on enterprise |

## 3. What the guide does not cover

Everything in the pathway's tiers is authored: creativity as a skill, divergent and convergent thinking, creative methods, cognitive flexibility, and enterprise. The guide's research example on creativity in education is a ready subject example for an education context.
