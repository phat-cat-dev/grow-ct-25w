# How *Excelling at University* plugs into Self-Development

The guide has no chapter on this pathway, but graduate identity is in its title and runs through it as a thread: each literacy's value is its place in graduate identity. This map records where that thread touches the pathway's cards. Pages are the guide's, found by script from the Markdown copy's page markers; descriptions are ing's paraphrase.

**Chapters:** none of its own; the thread runs through Chapters 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 10 and 14.

## 3. What the guide does not cover

Personal values as distinct from integrity's values, value conflict, background and positionality, self-authorship and reflexivity. The guide's thread of graduate identity carries the Introductory and Intermediate tiers and Advanced's articulation and conduct; the top of the pathway is authored from outside the guide.
