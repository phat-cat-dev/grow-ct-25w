### Course outcomes (`crs-03_course-outcomes`)

1. **co1**: Explain why universities use exams and what an exam tests that other assessment forms do not.
2. **co2**: Distinguish between the different forms an exam can take and recognise why its form should shape your preparation.
3. **co3**: Recognise exam anxiety for what it is and explain why it points to the need for a revision strategy, not avoidance.
