- *Generic outcome [conceptual]:* The student can describe planning, drafting and reviewing as distinct stages needed to produce a complete first draft in a required form.
- *Generic outcome [applied]:* The student can plan a structure and produce a complete working draft of a document before refining it to match its required form.

**i3:** Find and correct errors in own writing by proofreading and editing
- *Benchmark (universal-reference, direct):* Graduates write in the forms the subject requires.
- *Generic outcome [conceptual]:* The student can distinguish correcting surface errors from checking a draft's structure against the form its writing is required to take.

**Tier reading:**

- *Practice:* Begins to apply the writing cycle of academic writing to familiar, well-defined tasks with guidance, works with sources and data they are given, and describes the first draft clearly.
