Academic practice, one move at a time

The same three ideas run through each walkthrough: steps enforce the order a process depends on, a fade hands your attention to what changed, and a mark that draws itself on is a tutor's hand showing you where to look. Together they make an expert's invisible process visible — and repeatable.

The fades and the step sequence are the teaching — they show the process, not just the result.
