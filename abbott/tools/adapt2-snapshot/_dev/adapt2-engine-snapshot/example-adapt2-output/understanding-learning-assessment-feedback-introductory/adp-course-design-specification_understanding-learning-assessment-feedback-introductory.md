### Course outcomes (`crs-03_course-outcomes`)

1. **co1**: Apply an understanding of how university learning works to your own study.
2. **co2**: Interpret your own assessment tasks and marking criteria.
3. **co3**: Act on feedback to improve your own work.
4. **co4**: Align your own academic practice with the learning, assessment and feedback cycle.

**courseOutcomeCountReason**

Four, not three: co1-co3 are the component themes (learning, assessment, feedback); co4 is integrative. The course's strategic point is that learning, assessment and feedback do not operate in isolation but as a connected cycle the learner aligns their own practice with. That synthesis is distinct from the three component outcomes and cannot be folded into any one of them.

The concept prose sits in the separate concept-definition and concept-context blocks.

activity-patterns/evaluate-versions realised as palette/text (prose contrast; palette/narrative weighed, not led): a third-person worked example contrasting a vague reading of an illustrative learning outcome with a broken-down one (spotting the active verb and what would count as demonstrating it), the weaker (vague) reading first, using the character-token cast. Authored as a generic structured-fields frame; subject fields filled at CXP. The illustrative outcome is fictional (marked illustrative in build notes). Grounded in the contrast shape at ch3/how-is-learning-designed (pp.35-36) and the outcomes-as-organiser principle at ch3/learning-framework (pp.39-41).
