Module 2 · Lesson 3

Designing for depth

Consistent courses don't come from matching colours by hand — they come from a system. This lesson is where that idea becomes something you can use.

You'll explore where colour, type and space come from in a token system, build a block that carries its own metadata, and see how that keeps a course coherent as it grows.

  • Estimated time 12 min
  • Level Intermediate

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Explain where colour, type and space come from in a token system.
  • Build a two-column block with per-column metadata.
  • Toggle a frontmatter section between visible and metadata-only.

Before you begin

  • You can read a CSS custom property, e.g. var(--accent).
  • You've completed Lesson 2 — The token cascade.

What you'll need

  • A recent piece of your own written feedback.
  • Ten quiet minutes.

What to expect

Three short activities with worked examples, then a two-question checkpoint so you can confirm the idea landed before moving on.

Completing this lesson

Work through each block to the checkpoint at the end. The checkpoint marks the lesson complete.

Support & accessibility
