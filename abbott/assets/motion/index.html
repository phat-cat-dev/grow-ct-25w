SVG motion assets

22 icon and typography motions, plus six transition studies. Individual SVGs contain their own animation CSS and need no fonts or scripts. Preview controls do not alter the downloaded originals.

Icons are original vector interpretations, not Google Material Symbols glyph exports. Text uses your system font. Standalone SVGs animate automatically; previews start paused and reduced motion always wins.
