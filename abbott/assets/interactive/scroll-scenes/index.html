01 / OBSERVE

Start with
what you notice.

Slow down long enough to see the situation clearly. What is happening, and what might you be missing?

02 / QUESTION

Make room for
a better question.

Look beyond your first explanation. Find the assumption that would change your next move if it turned out to be wrong.

03 / EXPLORE

Try something
small and useful.

Compare possibilities without needing a perfect answer. A modest experiment can reveal more than another round of guessing.

04 / ACT

Take the learning
back with you.

Choose one action you can explain, test and review. Decide what evidence will tell you whether it helped.
