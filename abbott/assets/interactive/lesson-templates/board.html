The water cycle

Water moves in a loop: it evaporates, condenses into cloud, then falls as rain. Nothing is lost — it just changes form.

Evaporate

The sun warms the sea; water rises as vapour.

Condense

Vapour cools high up and gathers into cloud.

Precipitate

Droplets grow heavy and fall as rain.
