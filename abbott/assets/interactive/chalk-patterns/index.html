Chalk drawing patterns

Reusable draw-on patterns. Choose a combination or segment count, then draw once or repeat. Finished artwork stays visible. Downloads and copied SVGs include animation styles and your current speed, repeat and arrow settings.

Playback is opt-in. Reduced motion shows finished drawings. Each cycle is one closed outline split into sequential parts.
