    <p class="note">Extra hue formulas: Tight analogous uses +15°, −15°, +30°; Wide analogous +60°, −60°, +120°; Wide split +135°, +225°, +180°; Rectangle +45°, +180°, +225°. Pentadic steps by 72° over five anchors; Hexadic by 60° over six. Extra colours cycle those anchors with lightness variations. Spectrum evenly divides the full circle by the selected count. Golden angle advances by 180 × (3 − √5) degrees per colour, wrapping at 360°. All formulas rotate with the foundation and work with 3–10 colours.</p>
